        Section 9 · Attack Vector Analysis & Security Mechanisms
        
Defense-in-Depth Against Web2 & Web3 Attack Vectors

      


      

        

          
            

              Attack Vector
              Threat Description
              Trust Net v10.0 Defense Mechanism
            

          
          
            

              DNS Hijacking / Spoofing
              Publishing another company's wallet in a malicious domain's TXT record or seizing an existing domain at the registrar.
              Calling setProfile() requires a cryptographic signature from the private key of the 0x... wallet. If a domain is hijacked, the attacker gets an empty profile with zero reputation, while the legitimate owner re-binds a new domain and keeps 100% of their Trust Score.
            

            

              SIWE Replay Attacks
              Intercepting an EIP-4361 signature to gain unauthorized access to private prompts and wholesale price lists.
              Single-use cryptographic nonce, strict binding to domain, chainId: 8453, and short-lived expirationTime inside the signed SIWE payload.
            

            

              Sybil / Rating Manipulation
              Creating thousands of bot wallets to artificially inflate Trust Score.
              Fixed $1 anti-spam fee per profile on Base + lineage graph weighting based on counterparty onchain history and certified independent auditor signatures.
            

            

              Trade Secret Leakage
              Competitors scraping bespoke pricing, volume discounts, and supply terms.
              Asymmetric X25519 encryption for Confidential mode combined with a free Base RPC check for an active mutual onchain handshake (hasActiveHandshake) prior to serving private data.
