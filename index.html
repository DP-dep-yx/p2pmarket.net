Pricing & economics

A flat $1 per contract and 0.1% on crypto settlement

No listing subscriptions and no hidden 15–30% marketplace fees. The micro-fee on Base keeps the vector registry spam-free and funds the indexing infrastructure.

Contract issuance & signing $1

A single anti-spam fee on Base for domain registration (setProfile()), an NDA/partnership, or a commercial contract. It keeps bots out and preserves a clean registry for AI agents.

Price fixing & crypto settlement + 0.1%

Charged only when a deal amount is set and settled in native USDC on Base through the TrustMarketsTrade.sol escrow contract. Post-paid invoices (SEPA / Net-30) carry no extra percentage.

AI search, MCP & SIWE RPC checks $0

Uses the user's own client-side models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor). Access checks via Base RPC view calls are free.

      Architectural security
      
Cryptographic domain binding, price privacy, and Base contracts

      

        The platform avoids heavyweight third-party oracles. Domain ownership is verified via DNS TXT, private prompts are sealed with SIWE (EIP-4361), and settlement is guaranteed by audited Solidity code.

Domain spoofing protection (DNS TXT + domainToOwner)

        

        

          
          
          
        

      


      

        The company owner publishes a TXT record with their EVM wallet in the domain's root DNS zone. When setProfile() is called, the smart contract computes keccak256(abi.encodePacked(_domain)) and rejects the transaction if the domain is already bound to another address.
